Where it
lands.

Five ways to mark the slot the strip snaps to, so you can see which day you'll get before you let go. The slot is the second box, where the selected day always sits. Every phone here works: drag the days, flick them, or tap one. Leave a phone alone and it demonstrates itself.

Two fixed dots, above and below the slot.

02Viewfinder

03Rail

01

Sight

A dot above and a dot below the second box, like the sights on a dial. At rest they're faint marks; while you drag they turn solid and the day under them gets an outline.

At rest
Two faint dots. Quiet but always there, so the slot is learnable.
Moving
Dots go solid and grow; the landing day is outlined.
Language
Pure dot. The smallest addition of the five.
Risk
Easy to miss on a bright screen at rest.

02

Viewfinder

Four corner brackets frame the slot, like a camera's focus box. They only appear while the strip moves, sliding in from above and below, and fade once it settles.

At rest
Nothing. The strip looks exactly as it does today.
Moving
Brackets close in around the slot; the days pass through them.
Language
Line, not dot. Reads clearly whatever the day looks like.
Risk
Brackets touch the neighbouring boxes on the narrow desktop layout.

Variant
03

Rail

A dotted rail runs above and below the strip, the same dots as the timeline's gutter rail. The segment over the slot is darker, and while moving the whole rail brightens and that segment turns solid.

At rest
A faint rail with the slot's segment picked out.
Moving
Rail brightens; the slot segment thickens to solid dots.
Language
Echoes the gutter rail, so the strip and timeline feel related.
Risk
The full-width rail adds some visual noise at rest.

Variant
04

Readout

While moving, the weekday you'd land on is spelled out above the slot and a dot caret points up from below. The big date in the header previews it too, dimmed until you let go.

At rest
Only the caret, half strength.
Moving
Weekday label above, caret solid, header previews the day.
Language
Mono label and dot caret; the header preview matches how typing a day previews it.
Risk
The most text; needs the most room above the strip.

Variant
05

Tape

Each day carries a small tick above and below, so the ticks travel with the days like a tape measure past a fixed notch. You see the motion and the target together.

At rest
Just the two notches.
Moving
Ticks appear on every day and slide past the notch; the notch lengthens.
Language
Instrument-like, close to the duration and warning tracks in the sheet.
Risk
Ticks and notch can look alike mid-flick.
