03

Rules

Ringing and remembering are separate. The stored occurrence stays the oldest open one; the next one to ring is worked out from the clock on each device.

Ringing
A series whose stored occurrence has passed also arms its next future occurrence and that occurrence's warnings. Past ones are never re-fired, so a long absence can't cause a burst.
Synced
The oldest open occurrence, as today, plus a short list of occurrences already done ahead of it. Only your actions write these.
Derived
The missed count and the next armed occurrence. Every device computes them the same way, so nothing is written behind your back.
Never
A miss is never cleared by time passing, on any device.
Kinds
Reminders, alarms and markers alike.
Linux
Delivery, alarms included, still needs the running tray app.
